Pin maps and flash partitions are from slicemk/zmk, commit
71f8242b8ab2e9f9c86ebbb221ba15876413f295 (MIT license):
https://github.com/slicemk/zmk/tree/71f8242b8ab2e9f9c86ebbb221ba15876413f295/app/boards

Migrated to Zephyr 4.1 board metadata and upstream ZMK BLE splits. Both
revisions use nRF52833, not nRF52840. Preserve the vendor bootloader layout:
application 0x1000..0x6cfff, NVS 0x6d000..0x73fff, bootloader from 0x74000.
202205 uses the internal low-frequency RC oscillator; 202207 uses a crystal.

The shield preserves the vendor's 77 positions (the first is the boot button),
including the right matrix's seven-column offset. Flash matching upstream
central and peripheral builds together; the vendor peripheral firmware uses
a different split implementation. Clear old bonds when migrating.
